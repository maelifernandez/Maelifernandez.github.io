About

Maëli Fernandez

I hold a Master's in Communication, Marketing, Events & Public Relations from INSEEC Lyon, and chose to complete it with six months in an event agency, where I handle bid responses, sourcing and event coordination.

I love learning and I'm always looking to improve what already exists, whether it's a way of working, a proposal or the flow of an event. That's what I enjoy about events: every project is different, calls for quick adaptation and teaches me something new.

Eight months in the United States gave me a taste for working internationally, and that's the direction I'm building my career in.
