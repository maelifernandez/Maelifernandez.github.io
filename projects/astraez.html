Context

Astraez is a web platform I created from scratch to help teach English in French schools. It's openly in development — a project I run end to end, from product vision to build.

The vision

Give French schools a clear, engaging way to teach English online — structured for the classroom, simple for teachers, and consistent from one lesson to the next.

What it shows

  • Initiative — I identified a need and started building rather than waiting.
  • Digital skills — designing and building a web product hands-on.
  • Solo project management — scoping, prioritising and shipping on my own.
