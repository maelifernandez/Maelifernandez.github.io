This site has no contact form, no account and no newsletter. The only personal data I receive is what you choose to send me by email. I use it only to reply to you and never share it.

Visitor statistics

I use Umami to count visits: pages viewed, approximate country, type of device and the site the visit comes from. Umami sets no cookies and does not identify visitors: the statistics are anonymous, aggregated, and kept for six months at most. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal, your visit is not counted.

This site sets no cookies. Your language choice (EN/FR) is saved in your own browser only, so the site stays in your language. It is never sent to me.
